# Champions of the Rift

**Exploring champion win rates in professional League of Legends games played in 2026.**

Our central question is: **How do champion win rates differ across professional League of Legends games played in 2026?**

This project uses Oracle's Elixir esports match data. The supplied file contains 119,364 records from 9,947 games dated January 1 through November 20, 2026. Our champion analysis uses the 99,470 player records and excludes team summary rows.

We compare champion appearances and win rates, examine missing early-game statistics, and test the association between champion lineups and game outcomes. Our initial prediction model uses champion, role, and side to predict a player's team outcome before gameplay. It achieves 53.50% accuracy on 1,990 held-out games, compared with a 50% dummy reference.

This website currently presents a project overview for the checkpoint. The full report will be added as the project is completed. Observed win rates describe associations and do not establish that choosing a champion causes a team to win.
